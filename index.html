What it stands on

In 2024 a ten-year-old, Joe Nagai, presented work in Kyoto: swallowtails trained to avoid a scent as caterpillars still avoided it as butterflies. Untrained animals split 50/50 between the arms of a Y-maze; trained ones avoided the scented arm about 70% of the time. He had found Martha Weiss's moth work — a learned aversion surviving pupation — and repeated its shape.

The standard protocol uses ethyl acetate, a killing agent. He replaced it with lavender oil because it was pleasanter for animals he considered friends, and tested the shock levels on his own arm first.

In this film lavender is the trauma cue. The mercy and the injury are the same molecule. Nothing in the film says this out loud and everything in it depends on it.

The title is 莊周夢蝶 — Zhuang Zhou dreams he is a butterfly, wakes, and does not know which he is — with the doubt taken out. And the departure: the real experiment has exactly two outcomes and both are choices between two arms. The animal in this film produces a third that is in neither dataset. It refuses both and draws on the ceiling.

Elements and compounds

The world is a small number of primitives that combine, and fifty-seven compounds that are combinations of them — not a metaphor applied afterwards, but how the repository is built. A scene imports two or three assets, declares one of ten motions, names one of five plans, lists its cues. So BF-17 is not "a scene". It is:

Mi·St·Ml·Ki·Io·Ho·Rm·T1·Co·Se

a mill, a stage, the mill's set module, the creature kit, Iona, a HOLD, the mill's room tone, the arrival theme, a coat and a footstep. Written that way you can see that five other units are the same columns with one substitution — a fact about the film that watching it will not tell you.

Run it

git clone https://github.com/hartswf0/butterfly-halfworld
cd butterfly-halfworld
node harness/serve.mjs        # → http://127.0.0.1:8181/

No build step and nothing is bundled. The server exists only because a browser will not import an ES module over file://; the page imports the film's actual modules and runs them. Change a module, reload, and the atlas changes.

The mistakes

Kept in the write-up because they are the most useful thing there. In four passes of work the pattern held every single time: every serious defect produced a plausible picture, and every one was caught by looking at an image rather than by a test.

  • The close-up puppet came back long-jawed and I blamed the jaw. The eyes were half a head too high — everything below them had lengthened to compensate.
  • A scalloped shape appeared on three faces and never on the fourth. I read it as a hairstyle bug and rewrote the hair code twice. It was the jaw-width parameter overshooting into an anvil.
  • The frame instrument reported one of the best shots in the film as blank. Its single sample had landed inside an authored whiteout.
  • Told the frames were empty, I pushed the lens in and made two of them worse. Zooming does not create ink; there was nothing behind the subject to get closer to.
  • A constraint asks you to say less. Three separate times I answered by saying the same amount more carefully.
Eight ink levels. No photographs, no footage, no samples — every image is code.
Aeolian at the start, Dorian at the end. One note different. 物化.
