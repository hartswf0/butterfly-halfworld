## What it was for

THE CUT SHEET — WYGWYL × halfworld
==================================

Two cuts, both carved from the same 135-shot archive.

  A. THE PQ REEL   — 21 shots. Every genuinely HDR frame the archive holds, kept
                     HDR: HEVC 10-bit, BT.2020, SMPTE-2084. Stream-copied, so the
                     transfer curve survives the cut. No generation loss.
  B. THE WINDOW AND THE DOOR
                   — 135 shots, each used exactly once, routed to the stanza whose
                     text they actually depict, across all 111 stanzas of the suite.

Classification here is BY EYE, from extracted mid-frames — not from the archive's
own `visual_category` field. That field is a garbage bucket: it swallowed 68 of
135 shots, including both title cards and all five credit slates. See LEDGER.md.

Halfworld laws this cut obeys:
  · dot law         hard cuts only. A dissolve is two substances briefly coexisting;
                    this world does not have alpha.
  · blocking law    a shot is staged by NAME (a stanza id). No shot lands anywhere
                    the world has no station for it. An unknown name throws.
  · measure the ink every duration below was probed from the file, never assumed.
  · BREAK           the one permitted discontinuity, declared where it is used:
                    14_S4, THE REEL.
