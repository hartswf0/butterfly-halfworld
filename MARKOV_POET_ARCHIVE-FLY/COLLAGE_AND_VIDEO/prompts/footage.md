# footage

`cut/out/footage`

Made by `cut/leads.py`.

## What it was for

poem -> [(patch, 96)] × 8. One bar per picture, cycling the poem's distinct
    pictures until the phrase is full.

    A bar is 4.000s and the shortest source in the archive is 5.04s, so nothing
    has to loop inside a slot — the first build of this gave a 5s take a 10.7s
    slot and it restarted mid-shot, which reads as a dropout. Cycling instead
    gives every lead the same one-bar cut rhythm, which is what lets them
    intercut with each other and with the drawing side without anything being
    re-timed.
