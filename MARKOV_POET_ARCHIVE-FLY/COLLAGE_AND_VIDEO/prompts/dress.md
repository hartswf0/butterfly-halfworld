# dress

`cut/out/dress`

Made by `cut/dress.py`.

## What it was for

DRESS — the whole thing, as sequence: every part skinned or kept, every body cast
by what it is doing, and the bindings held from frame to frame.

    the halfworld  ->  blocking, timing, staging, and the names of its own pieces
    the archive    ->  photographed windows, walls, staircases, water, people
    the dictionary ->  cut/lexicon_parts.py, 22 words translated, 87 kept drawn
    the fit        ->  Hungarian per noun, on shape not appearance
    the binding    ->  a slot keeps its skin as long as the slot exists

BODIES ARE CAST BY WHAT THEY ARE DOING. The cast reports mode per body per frame,
and until now every body was fitted against one generic descriptor. A standing
figure is narrow, a walking one wider, a sitting one wider and shorter. And a
FALLING one is drawn from "a person in mid-air" — the phrase I nearly deleted for
firing on every shot in the archive. In a suite about leaving that promiscuity was
never noise; it is 57 photographed bodies not touching the ground, and THE FALL is
where they belong.

    python3 cut/dress.py 06 14
    python3 cut/dress.py 06 14 --loose
