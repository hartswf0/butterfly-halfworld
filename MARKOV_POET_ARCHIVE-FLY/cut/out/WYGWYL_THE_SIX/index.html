THE SIX

Not the suite. The archive was clustered on what the pictures are — palette, luma, and how much they move — and it fell into six worlds. Each got a song whose measured tempo matches its measured motion, and one cutting law it obeys the whole way through. A world is allowed to come back round to its own pictures: a three-minute film about one room that shows the room thirty times is not repeating itself, that is the film.
