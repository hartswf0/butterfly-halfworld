Archive
    
The Lens films what is in front of you. This is what was already
    filmed — the MARKOV POET archive, cut apart by a segmenter and measured. A piece

Halfworld
    
The drawn worlds are not pictures — they are programs, and every

How to run this

  

    

      
THE LOOP — five moves, bottom bar

      
    
        
  1. A · AUTHOR — capture, draw, write, or prompt a frame. Add a frame to keep going.
    2. 
        
  2. B · BRANCH — tap a node to point at it. Fork to make an alternate take.
    3. 
        
  3. C · CREW — bring people in. Joining needs no camera; the roster shows who is in the room and who is linked.
    4. 
        
  4. D · DIRECT — arrange every frame on the light table. Drag to reorder. The first reorder forks a TWIN so your cut never touches the original branch.
    5. 
        
  5. E · IN/OUT — publish the DIRECT cut on its own, or export the whole film.
    6. 
      

    


    
THE BOARD

      

        
Pen
Press and drag to lay dots. Pick a tone at the right of the tool row — left dots are light, right dots are heavy.

        
Box
Press one corner, drag to the other, release. Draws a rectangle outline in the current tone.

        
Erase
Press and drag to clear dots back to white.

        
Tone dots
The row of dots sets ink weight, light to dark. The swatch at the far right shows the tone you're holding.

        
Size slider
Sets how wide the pen draws. The frames-per-second slider sits next to PLAY on the timeline.

        
Play
Runs the current path as a flip. Press again to stop. Drawing also stops it.

      

    


    
THE LENS — film what is in front of you

      

        
Open it
The circle mark in the top bar, the camera mark on the timeline, or AUTHOR › Lens. The preview IS the film: live edges on the 192×144 grid.

        
Sources
WORLD and SELF steer the physical camera. FILE loads a photo or video. A peer chip aims the lens at that person’s incoming feed.

        
Tap the shutter
One frame of whatever the lens sees lands on your branch — a hand-cranked camera, source-blind.

        
Hold the shutter
Records a clip at the chosen fps (up to 32 frames), then drops the whole run on your branch.

        
No camera?
Load a photo or video, or pick a linked peer — it becomes frames the same way.

      

    


    
THE BREADCRUMB (above the board)

      

        
WORLD
The whole film. Tap it to aim the verbs at the top level.

        
act · scene · syntagm
The nesting under the world. Tap a level to point the verbs there.

        
frame a07
Where this frame sits in the run. The number is its position in the cut.

      

    


    
DIRECT — the light table

      

        
Five views
Grid, list, columns, board, outline — tap the icons top-left. Every one shows the frames as thumbnails.

        
Drag a frame
Reorders the cut as an overlay. The order sticks and plays back. The branch is not rewritten.

        
Tap a frame
Aims the board at that frame to edit it. Close DIRECT and you're drawing on it. The board shows its a07.

        
Play on a frame
Previews the cut from there on the board, then returns you to where you were editing.

        
DIRECT CUT
DIRECT shows the frames on the branch you're on — the same set as the timeline. Enter a different branch and DIRECT re-scopes to it. Your reorder is an overlay: the order sticks, but the branch itself is never rewritten.

        
Branch this cut
Forks your arranged order into a new branch line and drops you on its tip in the editor, so you can diverge and author more. The timeline marks lines that came from a cut.

        
Crumb
Tap WORLD to see the whole film; tap a level to narrow DIRECT to that branch.

      

    


    
TOP BAR

      

        
The Lens
The circle mark. One instrument, four sources — WORLD, SELF, FILE, or a linked peer’s feed. The shutter is source-blind.

        
Mic / Camera / Chat
Crew chrome. It renders only when a second person is in the room — the width of the bar is the social state. Rooms work without a camera.

        
Connect
Lives in C · CREW on the bottom bar — its one primary action.

        
?
Open this sheet. The event log lives in its footer.

      

    


    
THE GALLOP — the founding experiment

      

        
The bet
1878, Stanford v. the naked eye: does a galloping horse ever have all four hooves off the ground? Trace the ghost, string four frames, press PLAY — then tap the frame that answers a question no single frame can. That is the whole thesis of this tool.

        
Gates
Each part of the interface appears the first time you perform its verb: trace → the strip, four frames → PLAY, the bet → the tabs, a fork with ink on it → DIRECT, a reorder → IN/OUT. Gates persist on this device; an invite link opens everything.

        
Replay
The button below resets the gates and summons the ghost again. Your film is untouched — traced frames append to the current branch.

      

    


    
IN / OUT

      

        
Bring in
Open a saved project, scene pack, BEFLIX, or Twee.

        
Publish the DIRECT cut
Ships your arranged DIRECT sequence — as GIF, WEBM, Flipbook, or Twee. The branch topology is left alone.

        
Export the whole film
Saves or renders the entire tree, all branches.
