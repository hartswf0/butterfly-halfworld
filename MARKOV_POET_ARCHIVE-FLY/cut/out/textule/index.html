TEXTULE

textura + module. Not a tubelet — a tubelet is an object moving through time. This is a knot in a fabric that can be unpicked from the side.

Do not bind photographs to temporary dots. Bind them to a persistent surface underneath the marks. Motion is the carrier. Collage is the skin. BEFLIX is the scaffold, aperture, mortar and fallback.

WHY EVERY EARLIER ATTEMPT FAILED

Every compositor built before this bound imagery to raster. A slot's cells, a pane's cells. Cells are frame-local: at t and t+1 a walking body occupies a different set of them, so every binding was destroyed by motion. That was hidden by rendering stills, or by re-fitting each frame — which is the same as having no binding.

But the dots are emitted by a rig. solve(h, pose, stocky, guise) returns thirteen named joints, and drawFigure maps them into the field with one documented transform. That rig is the same object at every t. The persistent surface was already there; nothing needed to be invented, only exposed.

STEP 0 — the test that decided the theory

A coordinate (bone, u, v) must name the same place on the body at every pose. Measured, not assumed: 90 fixed body-space samples across full cycles.

ONE COLLAGE, EVERY POSE

Five sockets — head, torso, armA, armB, legs — each holding one SAM cutout, bound once in body space and never rebound. The same five fragments across a walk, a stand, a sit and a fall. The trousers swing because they were never on the legs' pixels; they were on the legs.

THE MORTAR AND THE SCAR

Coverage is never assumed. Every frame, every cell the figure occupies is asked for its body coordinate; where the skin has a hole or the coordinate falls outside a socket, the cell becomes mortar — a BEFLIX mark. Roughly 40% of every figure is mortar, and that is not a defect being tolerated. It is the seam being reported.

And a mortar cell touching a painted one is a seam, which is the only site of colour in the system. Amber, on an otherwise achromatic field. Colour is a report of damage rather than decoration — the repair finished in gold, the break made the most valuable surface. Look at any frame: the figure is outlined in the history of its own failure to be covered.

WHAT THE CODE DOES NOT CAPTURE

  • Why binding to dots is forbidden becomes invisible once the system works, precisely because it works. The prohibition is written where someone would break it.
  • The gusset. Time as a dressmaker who only does alterations — take in a seam, let out a dart, add a gusset. No code here contains the answer to what a gusset in time is; there is only a place where that operation would go.
  • Coverage is a diagnostic, never a goal. A maintainer optimising for it will make the seams disappear and destroy the work. What holds me together is what marks my dispersal.
