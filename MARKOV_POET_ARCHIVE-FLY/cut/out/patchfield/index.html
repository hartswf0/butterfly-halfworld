PATCHFIELD

Carriers of two kinds, skins of many, and layers that mix.

TEXTULE proved the binding on one carrier: a halfworld rig, whose thirteen named joints give a body chart surviving every pose. But the theory's own Change Test said it only holds if <Surface> is an interface rather than that one class — if a photographic region tracked through video satisfies the same invariant. This is that test.

TWO CARRIERS, ONE INTERFACE

RigCarrier — halfworld figuresolve() joints10exact
TrackCarrier — SAM 2 mask trackimage moments1approximate

A tracked blob has no joints. What it has is a mask per frame, and a mask has a centroid and a principal axis — which is a bone. So a TrackCarrier is a one-bone rig whose bone is the body's long axis: u runs head-to-foot, v across. The sign is resolved by the end with less mass, because a person is heavier at the hips than the head and that is more reliable than assuming up is up.

Crude, and crude is correct. The surface should be exactly as rich as the carrier's actual structure and no richer. Where the halfworld knows where an elbow is, we know where an elbow is. Where a tracked photograph does not, we do not pretend.

THE RICHER VOCABULARY

  • Banded skins. A skin occupies a range u0..u1 of the body, so a skeleton sits on the torso while a face sits on the head — several skins, one carrier.
  • Seven blend modes — normal, multiply, screen, overlay, burn, dodge, difference. Categorical, never interpolated: there is no tween from multiply to dodge, so a change of mode is a held keyframe or a crossfade of two parallel branches.
  • Screened skins. A skin arrives already through the dot law, so a photograph laid on a photograph still reads as a mark rather than a patch.
  • Mortar and scar, carried over: any cell inside the body that no skin reached is a seam, and a seam is the only site of colour in the system.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT YET DO

  • Carrier quality is the bottleneck now, not binding. A one-bone surface is fine for a standing or walking body and wrong for a body that folds. Multi-part tracking — SAM 3 prompted per limb — would give a tracked photograph more than one bone.
  • No mesh nodes. A socket is still one flat skin with no corrective keyframes, which is why an elbow reads bluntly.
  • No federated clock. head=f40, torso=f12, arm=live remains unrenderable. That is invariant I5 and it is the most radical piece left.
