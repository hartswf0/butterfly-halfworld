THE LAB — FINDING THE BODY

The poet kept coming back as an outline around a blank core. The cause was arithmetic: local contrast measured against a ground narrower than the figure is zero in the interior by construction, so no gain could ever fill a torso. The fix was a second layer — a body mass burned in under the drawing — and then the question became how to find that mass.

Five candidates, on six test cases. THRESH is the tone mass, which finds the biggest dark thing and cannot tell a poet from a couch. GRABCUT takes that as a hint. The last three are Segment Anything, prompted three ways. ENSEMBLE runs eleven prompts against one encoding and then selects, because a single prompt returned the entire frame on the neon shot and the page rendered solid grey.

What the selection asks of a proposal

AREA 3–45% of frame · BORDERS touching all four edges is the background · BBOX a room fills its bounding box and a person does not (past 0.82 it is architecture) · SOLIDITY a figure is one filled thing · CONFIDENCE the model's own predicted IoU · AGREEMENT proposals that concur are probably right. It is allowed to answer nothing — a landscape has no body, and inventing one there is how the threshold put ink on a couch.
