CINEOSIS-COLLAGE-001

The whole archive laid flat has no centre, so it perceives without a perceiver.

gaseous perception · CINEOSIS-010-03
TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPTNo protagonist and no horizon. Two thousand small photographic fragments hang in a pale field and drift at different speeds — a hand, a window, a strip of neon, a shoulder. Nothing enters and nothing exits. Bands of density slowly migrate upward like weather. Let fragments rhyme by shape rather than meaning: an ear answers a doorway, a lamp answers an eye. Never let the camera settle long enough for any fragment to become the subject. The film ends when the field has redistributed itself, not when anything has happened.
