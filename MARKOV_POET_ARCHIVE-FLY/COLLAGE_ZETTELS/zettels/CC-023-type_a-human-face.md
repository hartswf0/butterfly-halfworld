PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The dividual is the conjunction of collective and individual affects: a figure remains distinguishable while affect circulates through a mass.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
One noun of the segmenter's vocabulary, as a picture.

LOCAL MOVE:
A blue-brown composite in which one central face is almost resolved, several partial faces crowd it, and at the right edge one woman's face survives nearly intact and in focus. The stack failed to erase her, and that failure is the image.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The average face is unreadable; the one that resisted averaging is the one that carries the affect.

QUESTION:

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A face slowly assembling from many faces. Begin with an unreadable blue-brown mass. Over four minutes let features arrive from different people — one person's eyes, another's mouth, another's jaw — never settling into a single face, always nearly one. At the right edge of frame, one whole woman's face remains sharp and unmerged throughout, watching. Do not cut to what she sees. End when the centre almost becomes her and does not.

TENSION:
The face has historically been privileged as affect's surface, which can narrow affect to the anthropomorphic.

MISSING:
A way to see the segmenter's prior separately from the world it was looking at.

BOUNDARY:
An average ≠ the sign it resembles.
