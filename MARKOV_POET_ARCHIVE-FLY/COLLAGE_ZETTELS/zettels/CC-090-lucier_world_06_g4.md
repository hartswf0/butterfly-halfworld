ZETTEL

SOURCE:

cut/lucier.py: segment the image, reassemble it from its own parts, then segment
the reassembly, and repeat. After Alvin Lucier, *I Am Sitting in a Room*.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Gaseous perception removes the privileged centre so images act directly upon images.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The 4th pass of an image through the segmenter that describes it.

LOCAL MOVE:
Three sparse vertical drifts on cream. A viewer who did not know would not say people. The relations between fragments are all that is left, and they are enough to imply three of something.

SOURCE TERMS:
gaseous perception
recurrence
generation loss
resonance

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The process was intended as decay and instead reads as a taxonomy. Each
generation is a recognisable position in the perception series, in order,
without anyone having chosen the order.

QUESTION:
Is the segmenter's failure the same operation as Lucier's room?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does every recursive description walk the same path from solid to gaseous?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A pale field with three faint vertical drifts of small coloured fragments. No one is present. The fragments vary against one another — a dark one answers a dark one across the gap, a pale cluster thins as another thickens. Three presences are implied by the arrangement alone and never by anybody.

TENSION:
Generation loss is a property of the instrument, so the "taxonomy" may be a
portrait of the segmenter rather than of perception.

MISSING:
A control: the same recursion run with a different segmenter, to see whether the
path is the same.

BOUNDARY:
Degradation ≠ a sign, unless the degradation is doing the sign's work.
