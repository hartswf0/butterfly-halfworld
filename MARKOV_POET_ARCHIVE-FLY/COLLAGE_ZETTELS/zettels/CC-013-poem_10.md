ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/poem_10.webp — poem 10, MAGIC RIDE — 33 elements from 4 shots.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Any-space-whatever is the genetic affection-image: an indeterminate space capable of expressing nonhuman qualities and powers.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
A road at night, rendered as a horizontal band of yellow-green lamps with cut fragments floating along it. There is no vehicle, no driver and no destination — only the condition of transit.

SOURCE TERMS:
any-space-whatever
composition
affect
field

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The poem is called MAGIC RIDE and the collage contains no ride. What survived the cutting was the roadside.

QUESTION:
What must be removed from a place before it stops being a setting?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A night road with no car and no camera-in-a-car. Lamps recede in a yellow-green chain. Things pass through frame at the speed of a vehicle — a sign, a rail, a rectangle of light — but nothing is travelling. The road itself has the momentum. Hold until the absence of a traveller becomes the subject rather than an omission.

TENSION:
Motion cues without a mover can be read as a missing shot rather than an intention.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.

CITATION TRAIL:

TEST:
Remove half the elements at random. If the operation survives, the sign was
carried by structure rather than by density.
