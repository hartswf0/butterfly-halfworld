ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/poem_06.webp — poem 06, RESURRECTING ATLANTIS — 253 elements from 12 shots.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The seed-environment crystal couples a virtual/actual seed to an actual/virtual environment so each develops through the other.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
Pale blue and bone. Standing figures are stacked until they read as columns, and the columns read as a skyline. The people are the architecture and the architecture is only people.

SOURCE TERMS:
seed and environment
composition
affect
field

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The city was never photographed. It is an emergent property of stacking bodies, and it is nonetheless the most legible city in the suite.

QUESTION:
What makes a set of bodies a seed for a world rather than a crowd inside one?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A pale underwater plaza whose columns, on inspection, are made of standing human figures pressed into one another. Move slowly along the colonnade. Each column occasionally resolves into a face and loses it again. As the camera passes, new columns assemble behind from figures arriving. The city grows out of the bodies and the bodies are held up by the city. Never give a wide shot that settles the question.

TENSION:
Reciprocity has to be shown; a mosaic portrait alone is only a technique.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.
