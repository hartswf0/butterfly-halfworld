ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/poem_05.webp — poem 05, BLOODLINES — 122 elements from 6 shots.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Strong destiny is recollection whose actualized past decisively reorganizes the present and future.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
A red ribcage is registered over a standing figure in bokeh. The bones are not inside the body; they are printed on it, in the same red as the lights behind. Inheritance rendered as an overlay.

SOURCE TERMS:
strong destiny
composition
affect
field

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Lineage is drawn as a compositing operation. The dead are a layer, not a memory.

QUESTION:
When does a flashback become causal rather than explanatory?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A woman stands in red night light. Over her torso, an X-ray ribcage in the same red, breathing very slightly out of time with her. As she moves, the ribcage lags. Cut once to an older woman standing in exactly the same posture in a different decade, then back. The ribcage now keeps the older woman's time. Her next action must be caused by what the overlay showed.

TENSION:
An overlay can read as graphic device rather than as the past acting.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.
