TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A single wall filling the frame for its entire duration. Light crosses it, damp rises through it, paint lifts, a shadow of something unseen passes. At the bottom edge, small coloured things accumulate and are cleared away. Nothing is in front of the wall. The wall must acquire tension, expectancy and power without anyone arriving to give it any.

TENSION:
A textured surface can be photographed lovingly and still remain decor.
