ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/lucier/world_06_g8.webp — generation 8 of a frame of the drawn world 06 RESURRECTING ATLANTIS, produced by
cut/lucier.py: segment the image, reassemble it from its own parts, then segment
the reassembly, and repeat. After Alvin Lucier, *I Am Sitting in a Room*.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The lectosign is the limiting read-image: visible fragments demand virtual relinking through interpretation.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The 8th pass of an image through the segmenter that describes it.

LOCAL MOVE:
The final generation is almost empty. What remains cannot be perceived as a scene; it can only be read against everything that came before it.

SOURCE TERMS:
lectosign
recurrence
generation loss
resonance

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The process was intended as decay and instead reads as a taxonomy. Each
generation is a recognisable position in the perception series, in order,
without anyone having chosen the order.

QUESTION:
Is the segmenter's failure the same operation as Lucier's room?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does every recursive description walk the same path from solid to gaseous?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

An almost empty pale field with a handful of marks. Nothing in the frame completes itself. Hold it far longer than is comfortable. The viewer must reconstruct three people, a room and a light from marks that no longer depict any of them — and must do the reconstructing themselves, because the image will not.

TENSION:
Generation loss is a property of the instrument, so the "taxonomy" may be a
portrait of the segmenter rather than of perception.

MISSING:
A control: the same recursion run with a different segmenter, to see whether the
path is the same.

BOUNDARY:
Degradation ≠ a sign, unless the degradation is doing the sign's work.
