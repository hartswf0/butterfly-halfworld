ZETTEL

the reassembly, and repeat. After Alvin Lucier, *I Am Sitting in a Room*.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
A demark is an aberration inside a habitual image-series whose meaning depends on the rule it breaks.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The 6th pass of an image through the segmenter that describes it.

LOCAL MOVE:
At this generation the image cannot be read alone. Its content is entirely the difference between it and generation five.

SOURCE TERMS:
demark
recurrence
generation loss
resonance

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The process was intended as decay and instead reads as a taxonomy. Each
generation is a recognisable position in the perception series, in order,
without anyone having chosen the order.

QUESTION:
Is the segmenter's failure the same operation as Lucier's room?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does every recursive description walk the same path from solid to gaseous?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Two nearly identical near-empty fields, side by side, from consecutive generations. The film is the difference between them. Show the earlier, then the later, then the earlier again. Everything the image means is in what has gone.

TENSION:
Generation loss is a property of the instrument, so the "taxonomy" may be a
portrait of the segmenter rather than of perception.

MISSING:
A control: the same recursion run with a different segmenter, to see whether the
path is the same.

BOUNDARY:
Degradation ≠ a sign, unless the degradation is doing the sign's work.
