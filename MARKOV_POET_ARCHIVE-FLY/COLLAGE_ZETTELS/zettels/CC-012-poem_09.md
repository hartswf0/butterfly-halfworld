ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/poem_09.webp — poem 09, YET, HEARD — 32 elements from 7 shots.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The icon is the face as expression of an internal intensity that cannot itself be directly filmed.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
Two dark figures overlap in front of a hard radial burst. No features survive — the light has eaten them — and yet the image is entirely about what passes between them.

SOURCE TERMS:
icon
composition
affect
field

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The icon here has no face. Intensity is carried by silhouette and by the light behind it.

QUESTION:
What distinguishes a face carrying information from a face functioning as affection-image, when there is no face?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Two people face each other against a hard blue radial burst that blows out every feature. Hold. Do not cut to what either of them sees, and never light their faces. The entire event is the minute change in the gap between the two silhouettes as the burst breathes. When the light finally drops enough to show a face, end the shot immediately.

TENSION:
Withholding the face can read as a stylistic refusal rather than as affect.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.

CITATION TRAIL:

TEST:
Remove half the elements at random. If the operation survives, the sign was
carried by structure rather than by density.
