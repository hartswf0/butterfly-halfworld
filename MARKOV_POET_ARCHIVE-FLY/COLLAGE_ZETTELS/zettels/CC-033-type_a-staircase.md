The index of equivocity reveals a situation through action but preserves ambiguity about what that situation actually is.

RESEARCH OBJECT:

LOCAL MOVE:
A hot orange flight of steps sits beside a dark industrial stair-frame at a different scale and angle. Ten instances were too few to reconcile, so the composite holds two incompatible staircases at once.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:

QUESTION:

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Two staircases in one space, at incompatible angles and scales — one hot orange and domestic, one dark and industrial. A figure climbs. Depending on which stair the camera favours, the same ascent reads as escape or as descent into a plant. Every new action supports both. Never establish which building this is, and never let the two stairs be seen to meet.

TENSION:
A visual pun can produce ambiguity that is decorative rather than situational.

MISSING:
A way to see the segmenter's prior separately from the world it was looking at.

BOUNDARY:
An average ≠ the sign it resembles.
