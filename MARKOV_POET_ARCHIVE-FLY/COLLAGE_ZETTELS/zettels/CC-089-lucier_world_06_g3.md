PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Liquid perception is the passage of perceptual privilege, not of the camera.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The 3th pass of an image through the segmenter that describes it.

LOCAL MOVE:
The figures are columns of drifting fragments. Attention no longer transfers between three people; it flows down each of them and across the gaps.

SOURCE TERMS:
liquid perception
recurrence
generation loss
resonance

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The process was intended as decay and instead reads as a taxonomy. Each
generation is a recognisable position in the perception series, in order,
without anyone having chosen the order.

QUESTION:
Is the segmenter's failure the same operation as Lucier's room?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does every recursive description walk the same path from solid to gaseous?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Three vertical currents of fragments where three people were. Attention runs down one, crosses the gap, and runs down the next, continuously, never stopping. No face resolves. The transfer has become the only event.

TENSION:
Generation loss is a property of the instrument, so the "taxonomy" may be a
portrait of the segmenter rather than of perception.

MISSING:
A control: the same recursion run with a different segmenter, to see whether the
path is the same.

BOUNDARY:
Degradation ≠ a sign, unless the degradation is doing the sign's work.
