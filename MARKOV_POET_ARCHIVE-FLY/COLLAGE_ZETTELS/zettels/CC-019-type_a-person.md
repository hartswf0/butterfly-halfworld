PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The dividual is the conjunction of collective and individual affects: a figure remains distinguishable while affect circulates through a mass.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
One noun of the segmenter's vocabulary, as a picture.

LOCAL MOVE:
The most-instanced noun in the archive produces the least specific image: a warm brown-mauve mound of shoulders and heads in which no one person survives, and which is unmistakably made of persons.

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A slow-moving mass of bodies, warm brown and mauve, so densely overlapped that no individual can be followed for more than a second. Occasionally one shoulder, one turn of a head, one hand becomes briefly distinct and is reabsorbed. The mass breathes as one. Never cut to a face. Feeling must travel across the whole body of it and never belong to anyone.

TENSION:
A crowd rendered as texture can erase individual affect rather than distribute it.

MISSING:
