PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The limpid–opaque crystal operates when actual and virtual exchange roles rather
than remaining stable original and reflection.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
an ordinary crossing at maximum saturation, transcribed from photograph into ink.

LOCAL MOVE:
This exists only as a reduction of the plate, so the plate is obviously the
original. But the dot field is the one the tool can actually take apart, bind
into, animate and re-render — so it is the one that behaves like a world, and the
photograph becomes its illustration.

QUESTION:

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is the reduced image more real than the photograph because it is more operable?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Two versions of one place, cut against each other: the photographic assemblage, and the same place in eight weights of black ink with a single blue. Begin by treating the photograph as real and the ink as its diagram. Then let the ink move first — a dot field shifts and, a beat later, the photograph catches up. Then let something appear in the ink that has no counterpart in the photograph and stay there. End with the photograph looking like a memory of the dots.

TENSION:
Mixing a photograph and its halftone can be pure graphic style with no exchange
of reality-status at all.

MISSING:
A necessary indicator that role reversal has actually occurred.

BOUNDARY:
Mixed media ≠ limpid and opaque.
