TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

One continuous take through water in all its states. Begin on rain hitting a road; follow the runoff to a gutter; follow the gutter to a river; the river's surface becomes a reflection; the reflection becomes a window with rain on it; the rain becomes spray from a passing vehicle. Attention is handed on at each transfer and never returns. No character and no destination — only the passage of perceptual privilege through one substance.

TENSION:
Camera movement alone does not make perception liquid; the privilege has to migrate.
