ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016 — Figure II.12, p. 95.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/BY_POEM.webp — the fourteen poem-collages as one sheet,
each captioned with its element and shot count.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The vector is the genetic sign of small-form action: multiple situations become
coordinates of one universe through what connects them.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Fourteen independently composed poem-fields, ruled into a four-column grid.

LOCAL MOVE:
No poem contains the suite. The grid does — and it does so by declaring the
disparity out loud. OUT OF LIFE is woven from 1,106 elements across 67 shots;
FLASHING LIGHTS from 16 elements across one. The common world is visible in the
caption line, not in the pictures.

SOURCE TERMS:
vector
multiple situations
homogeneous universe
trajectory

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The unity is administrative. A table of counts is doing the work a master shot
would do in a film.

QUESTION:
Is a grid a trajectory, or only the record of one?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a catalogue perform the vector, or must something move between the cells?

MECHANISM:
poem 01 … poem 14
→ identical frame, identical caption grammar
→ comparison becomes possible
→ one suite.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<FOURTEEN FIELDS>
→ <ONE RULE>
→ [COMPARE]
→ <ONE SUITE>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Vector is the genetic sign of the A → S action-image.

OUR FORMALIZATION:

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Fourteen panels on one screen, all running at once, each a different poem's
weather: teal drowned room, orange stairwell, red heart on a chest, olive arch,
skeleton in bokeh, pale blue columns, neon and a red coat, rain on glass, blue
starburst, night road, two men on white ground, grey tunnel, a man in smoke, red
figure in lights. Never cut to a single panel. Instead let one element cross a
frame edge into its neighbour — a light leaves 07 and arrives in 08, a figure
walks out of 11 and into 12. The suite is proved by what can traverse it.

TENSION:
Juxtaposition alone is not connection; the crossings have to be real.

MISSING:
A minimum connectivity condition for the fourteen to be one world.

BOUNDARY:
A contact sheet ≠ a vector.

TEST:
Remove the captions. If the fourteen stop reading as one suite, the unity was
in the counting.
