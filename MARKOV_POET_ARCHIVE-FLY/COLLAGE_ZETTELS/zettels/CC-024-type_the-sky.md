TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A sky that is all skies at once. A hot white core with pale blue radiating from it, crossed by orange streaks that behave like lens artefacts rather than light. Cloud forms assemble and are overrun by other cloud forms at a different scale. No horizon and no ground. Time of day changes continuously without ever passing through dusk. Nothing observes it.
