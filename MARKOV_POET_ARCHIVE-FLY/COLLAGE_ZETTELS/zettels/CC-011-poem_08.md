PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Opsigns and sonsigns are pure optical and sound situations left after the sensory-motor linkage ceases to organize the image.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
Everything is behind water. Streetlight and headlight are reduced to lozenges of colour; the only thing in focus is the glass and the drops on it. Nothing in the frame can be acted upon.

SOURCE TERMS:
opsign and sonsign

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The subject of the image is an obstruction. There is nothing to do about rain.

QUESTION:
Can an image with a clear point of view still be a pure optical situation?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Fixed shot through a rain-covered window for six minutes. Beyond it a street exists only as smeared colour. Drops accumulate, merge, run, and clear a momentary lens through which something almost resolves and is lost. No one wipes the glass. No one arrives. Sound is only rain, glass, and distantly a city that will not be reached. Nothing in the frame can be responded to.

TENSION:
Patient attention still selects; the window is a choice, and choices imply a chooser.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.
