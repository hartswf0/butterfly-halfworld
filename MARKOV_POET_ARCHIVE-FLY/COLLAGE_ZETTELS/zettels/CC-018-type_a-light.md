Every light the segmenter found, on black. Nothing anchors them — no lamp-post, no window frame, no street. They form drifts and clusters that read as a city and are not one.

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Pure black. Hundreds of small lights of every colour and shape drift at different rates — some flare, some are steady, some are the smeared trace of a light that moved during exposure. No lamp, no window, no street ever appears to explain any of them. Let them cluster into something that feels like a skyline, then let it disperse. The film is a city seen only as its emissions.

TENSION:
A field of lights on black is beautiful by default, which can substitute for the operation.
