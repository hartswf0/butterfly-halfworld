ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/poem_07.webp — poem 07, DJ TURN ME UP — 63 elements from 2 shots.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The dividual is the conjunction of collective and individual affects: a figure remains distinguishable while affect circulates through a mass.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
Two figures are cleanly cut and fully individual; behind them the neon is chopped into vertical slabs that behave as one body of colour. A third figure in red is half-dissolved into that mass.

SOURCE TERMS:
dividual
composition
affect
field

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The image holds all three states at once — cut out, half-absorbed, wholly collective — in one frame.

QUESTION:
How much individuation can a collective sustain and remain dividual?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A club street. Two men stand sharply separate in the foreground. Behind them the signage is not signage but a wall of vertical colour slabs that pulse together as one organism. A woman in red stands between the two conditions, her edges resolving and dissolving with the beat. Let the crowd's feeling arrive first in the slabs, then in her, and last in the two sharp men. No one leads.

TENSION:
Foreground/background separation can produce hierarchy rather than dividuation.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.

TEST:

carried by structure rather than by density.
