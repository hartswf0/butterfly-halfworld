the reassembly, and repeat. After Alvin Lucier, *I Am Sitting in a Room*.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
This chronosign continually ungrounds the image through serial narration rather than restoring one true account.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The 7th pass of an image through the segmenter that describes it.

LOCAL MOVE:
Fragments have begun to cluster into forms that resemble nothing in the original photograph. The series has stopped losing the source and started inventing.

SOURCE TERMS:
powers of the false
recurrence
generation loss
resonance

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The process was intended as decay and instead reads as a taxonomy. Each
generation is a recognisable position in the perception series, in order,
without anyone having chosen the order.

QUESTION:
Is the segmenter's failure the same operation as Lucier's room?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does every recursive description walk the same path from solid to gaseous?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Marks on a pale ground that arrange themselves into a figure that was never photographed. Cut between this and the original three people. The invented figure is more convincing than the record. Do not reveal which is the source. Each pass produces another candidate and none of them converge.

TENSION:
Generation loss is a property of the instrument, so the "taxonomy" may be a
portrait of the segmenter rather than of perception.

MISSING:
A control: the same recursion run with a different segmenter, to see whether the
path is the same.

BOUNDARY:
Degradation ≠ a sign, unless the degradation is doing the sign's work.
