PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
In the index of lack, the situation begins as an ellipsis. Actions progressively disclose what was absent.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
One noun of the segmenter's vocabulary, as a picture.

LOCAL MOVE:
Eight dark slabs scattered on cream, with enormous empty space between them. The category is so thinly populated that the composite is mostly absence, and the absence is the finding.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The archive filmed 135 shots and produced ten doors. The image is a report about what was not filmed.

QUESTION:
What differentiates an index of lack from ordinary exposition-by-investigation?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is a category an image, or only the shadow of the instrument that named it?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A pale empty room-sized field with a handful of doors standing in it, unattached to any wall. Someone opens the first: behind it, more of the same emptiness. They cross to the second and open it. Each opening reveals one more fact about a building that is not there — a draught, a sound, a change of floor. The situation is assembled entirely from what the doors imply and never shown.

TENSION:
If the missing situation is finally shown, the image resolves and stops being an index.

MISSING:
A way to see the segmenter's prior separately from the world it was looking at.

BOUNDARY:
An average ≠ the sign it resembles.
