ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/lucier/shot_P133_g1.webp — generation 1 of a frame of three figures from shot P133, produced by
cut/lucier.py: segment the image, reassemble it from its own parts, then segment
the reassembly, and repeat. After Alvin Lucier, *I Am Sitting in a Room*.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Solid perception is full composition: the world organises around a privileged perceiver.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The 1th pass of an image through the segmenter that describes it.

LOCAL MOVE:
The three figures are still legible and still dominant, but they are now assembled from fragments of themselves. The composition holds; the substance does not.

SOURCE TERMS:
solid perception
recurrence
generation loss
resonance

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The process was intended as decay and instead reads as a taxonomy. Each
generation is a recognisable position in the perception series, in order,
without anyone having chosen the order.

QUESTION:
Is the segmenter's failure the same operation as Lucier's room?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does every recursive description walk the same path from solid to gaseous?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

The same three people, still centred, still the subject — but their surfaces have begun to be made of small photographic fragments of themselves. Nothing about the framing changes. Only what they are made of.

TENSION:
Generation loss is a property of the instrument, so the "taxonomy" may be a
portrait of the segmenter rather than of perception.

MISSING:
A control: the same recursion run with a different segmenter, to see whether the
path is the same.

BOUNDARY:
Degradation ≠ a sign, unless the degradation is doing the sign's work.
