ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/poem_12.webp — poem 12, REUNION — 40 elements from 6 shots.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Sheets of the past present nonchronological layers of past that coexist and may be entered without proceeding in historical order.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
Six shots of a street are registered into one grey corridor. Figures appear as vertical smudges at several depths at once, none of them in the same moment as any other.

SOURCE TERMS:
sheets of the past
composition
affect
field

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The image contains several times and no sequence. Depth has become chronology.

QUESTION:
Is spatial navigation here already temporal navigation?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

MECHANISM:
one poem's shots
→ segmented into elements
→ laid over one another
→ a single field carrying the poem's operation.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<ELEMENTS>
→ <ACCUMULATION>
→ [COMPOSE]
→ <SHEETS OF THE PAST>

SOURCE FORMALISM:

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

One continuous slow move down a misty street. At each depth a different decade is in progress: nearest figures present-day, mid-ground twenty years back, the far end further still. No dissolves, no flashback cues. Walking forward is going backward. Let a figure from one layer step into another and be unremarked. The street is the archive.

TENSION:
Atmospheric haze can imply a single foggy day rather than coexistent layers.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[CINEOSIS-010-39]] → Deamer → poem 12.

TEST:
Remove half the elements at random. If the operation survives, the sign was
carried by structure rather than by density.
