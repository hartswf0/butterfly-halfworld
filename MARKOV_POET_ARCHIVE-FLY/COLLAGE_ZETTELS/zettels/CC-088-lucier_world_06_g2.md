PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Liquid perception flows from character or event to character or event. Centres form temporarily and yield.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The 2th pass of an image through the segmenter that describes it.

LOCAL MOVE:
The middle figure loses coherence first and attention slides to the outer two. No one holds the frame for long. The image has stopped having a subject and started having a sequence of them.

SOURCE TERMS:
liquid perception
recurrence
generation loss
resonance

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The process was intended as decay and instead reads as a taxonomy. Each
generation is a recognisable position in the perception series, in order,
without anyone having chosen the order.

QUESTION:
Is the segmenter's failure the same operation as Lucier's room?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does every recursive description walk the same path from solid to gaseous?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Three figures, none of whom can hold your attention for more than a moment. As one dissolves, another briefly firms up and takes the centre, then loses it in turn. The camera does not move. Perceptual privilege moves instead.

TENSION:
Generation loss is a property of the instrument, so the "taxonomy" may be a
portrait of the segmenter rather than of perception.

MISSING:
A control: the same recursion run with a different segmenter, to see whether the
path is the same.

BOUNDARY:
Degradation ≠ a sign, unless the degradation is doing the sign's work.
