ZETTEL

David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/poem_11.webp — poem 11, NEW DAY — 33 elements from 2 shots.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Gest arises when bodily attitudes are linked so that their conjunction produces a differentiation or thought that none contains alone.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
Two cleanly cut figures walk past each other on an empty white ground, a row of yellow columns behind. The background has been almost entirely removed. What is left is two gaits and a distance.

SOURCE TERMS:
body of gest
composition
affect
field

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Deleting the world made the walking legible. This is the only poem-field where posture is the content.

QUESTION:
How many attitudes must be linked before gest emerges?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

White void, a colonnade in yellow light far behind. Two men walk the same line in opposite directions. Repeat the crossing many times with small variations: one hesitates, one shortens his stride, one carries something, one does not look. Never explain their relation. The meaning must accumulate only from the series of crossings, and vanish if the crossings are reordered.

TENSION:
Isolated figures on white can read as design language rather than as gest.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.

TEST:

carried by structure rather than by density.
