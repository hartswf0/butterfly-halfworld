ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/poem_04.webp — poem 04, NEVERMORE — 16 elements from 5 shots.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Restrained dreams produce ambiguity between real and dream worlds rather than a clean transition.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
Olive and gold. A seated silhouette sits under a lit arch; a bottle occupies the lower foreground at a scale that cannot be right. The image never declares itself as vision or as room.

SOURCE TERMS:
restrained dream
composition
affect
field

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The impossible object is small and calm. Nothing announces that this is a dream, which is precisely what makes it one.

QUESTION:
What is the smallest disturbance sufficient to make an ordinary interior dreamlike?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Olive-gold interior, one lamp burning above an arch. A seated figure, back to us, does not move. In the foreground a bottle stands far too large, in focus, present. Change almost nothing for two minutes. Once, the arch light dims and returns a half-second late. Once, the bottle's reflection shows a room that is not this one. Never cut away and never wake anybody.

TENSION:
Without a waking world to compare against, restrained dream can collapse into atmosphere.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.
