PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Any-space-whatever is the genetic affection-image: an indeterminate space capable
of expressing nonhuman qualities and powers.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
a red interior where the bones are on the outside — assembled as a standable place rather than an accumulated field.

LOCAL MOVE:
Unlike the poem-collage, which stacks everything at one depth, the plate SORTS.
Sky is committed to the top, a horizon is declared, masses are given a footing.
The result is a location that no camera ever occupied and that a body could
nevertheless be placed into.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Sorting by depth was enough to turn a pile into a world. No perspective was
computed; only a decision about what belongs above what.

QUESTION:
What must be removed from a place for it to cease being merely setting?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a place assembled from non-adjacent fragments carry affect that none of the
fragments had?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A place that was never photographed. A red interior where the bones are on the outside. Hold it as a fixed frame with no one in it. Light crosses it from a source that could not exist given its geometry. Small things move at the wrong scale — something distant passes too fast, something near too slow. Do not resolve the perspective and do not send anyone in. The location must acquire expectancy on its own, and the film ends before it is used for anything.

TENSION:
A convincingly built place can slide back into establishing shot, which is
action-image territory.

MISSING:
A test that separates nonhuman affect from ordinary atmospheric mise-en-scène.

BOUNDARY:
An empty establishing shot ≠ any-space-whatever.

TEST:
Put a character in it with something to do. If the place immediately becomes
background, its affect was borrowed from its emptiness.
