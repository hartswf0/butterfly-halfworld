ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/poem_14.webp — poem 14, HOT MINUTE — 89 elements from 5 shots.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The quotidian joins ordinary situations and ordinary actions while allowing them to oscillate between immense significance and apparent futility.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
A figure in red stands in a field of soft city lights. Nothing is happening and the colour insists that something is. The image is simultaneously a snapshot and an emergency.

SOURCE TERMS:
quotidian
composition
affect

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The frame refuses to say whether this is a night out or the last night.

QUESTION:
What preserves quotidian scale when the palette is already at maximum?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A woman in a red coat stands at a crossing among ordinary city lights, waiting. She checks a phone. She shifts her weight. The light does not change. Keep the action entirely mundane and the colour entirely extreme. Somewhere off-frame something enormous is audible and never explained, and she does not react because she has heard it before. Give the burnt-out streetlamp as much attention as the sound.

TENSION:
Saturated colour can tip the everyday into spectacle before the ordinary has been established.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.

TEST:
