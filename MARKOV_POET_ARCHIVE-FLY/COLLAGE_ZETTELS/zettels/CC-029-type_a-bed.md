TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A low teal mound in the dark, breathing very slightly, filmed as landscape rather than furniture. Hold on it. Over time, the impressions of bodies that have lain there rise and fade in the surface — not ghosts, just depressions that fill in slowly, as if the material had memory and was slow to forget. No one is in it. No one comes.

TENSION:
Anthropomorphic bedding reads as horror convention; the imprint must be dispositional, not spooky.
