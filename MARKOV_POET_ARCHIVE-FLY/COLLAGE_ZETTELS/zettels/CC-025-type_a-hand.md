The symptom is a body overwhelmed by primal forces whose pressure has not yet stabilised into ordinary action.

RESEARCH OBJECT:

LOCAL MOVE:
Unlike the other composites this one did not merge. The hands stay separate, scattered across a mostly empty cream field, each frozen mid-gesture with nothing to act upon.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Hands refused to average. The one category that is defined by doing produced the one image with nothing being done.

QUESTION:
When does a gesture without an object become symptomatic rather than merely idle?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is a category an image, or only the shadow of the instrument that named it?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A pale empty field. Hands enter one at a time from nowhere, each already mid-gesture — gripping, pointing, reaching, cupping — hold, and leave. There is never anything to grip, point at, reach for, or hold. The gestures come faster. They begin to repeat. The film must make it clear that some force is moving these hands and that it is not intention.

TENSION:
Isolated body parts read as surrealist convention, which can domesticate the impulse.

MISSING:
