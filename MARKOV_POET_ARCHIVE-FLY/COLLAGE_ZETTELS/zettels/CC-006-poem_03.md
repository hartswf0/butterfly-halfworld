ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/collage/poem_03.webp — poem 03, HOW TO BREAK OFF AN ENGAGEMENT — 44 elements from 4 shots.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
A fetish is a special object saturated by primal energy before that energy receives ordinary situational grounding.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The accumulated elements of one poem, laid over one another as a single field.

LOCAL MOVE:
A red-lit figure stands in bokeh with a pale heart shape laid over the sternum. The heart is not anatomy and not costume — it is a mark the collage put there. Everything else in the frame is soft; only it has an edge.

SOURCE TERMS:
fetish
composition
affect
field

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The collage invented an object that was in none of the four shots and gave it all the charge.

QUESTION:
When a composite adds an object, is that object a fetish or an argument?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does a still made by accumulation already contain its own duration?

MECHANISM:
one poem's shots
→ segmented into elements
→ laid over one another
→ a single field carrying the poem's operation.

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A man in red half-light, city bokeh behind him. Over his sternum a pale heart-shape sits like a decal, slightly wrong in scale, refusing to move with his breathing. Everything soft-focus except the heart, which stays hard-edged. Push in very slowly. Other people pass and none of them look at it. Do not explain it. End before anything happens to it.

TENSION:
A symbol and a fetish can look identical in a still; only what the film does with it separates them.

MISSING:
A measure of when accumulation stops adding and starts erasing.

BOUNDARY:
A layered still ≠ the sign it resembles.

CITATION TRAIL:

TEST:
Remove half the elements at random. If the operation survives, the sign was
carried by structure rather than by density.
