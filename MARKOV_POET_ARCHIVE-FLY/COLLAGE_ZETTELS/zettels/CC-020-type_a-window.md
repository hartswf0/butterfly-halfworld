RESEARCH OBJECT:
One noun of the segmenter's vocabulary, as a picture.

LOCAL MOVE:
Teal rectangles at many scales, overlapping into an interior that no building has. What survives the averaging is not a window but the relation between an opening and a room.

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

An interior assembled only from openings. Teal rectangles of daylight at incompatible scales hang in a dim room whose walls are never shown. Light moves across them as if from one sun, though they cannot share one. Nobody enters. Hold long enough that the room's expectancy becomes its own event.

TENSION:
Layered rectangles can read as graphic abstraction rather than as an indeterminate place.

MISSING:
A way to see the segmenter's prior separately from the world it was looking at.

BOUNDARY:
An average ≠ the sign it resembles.
