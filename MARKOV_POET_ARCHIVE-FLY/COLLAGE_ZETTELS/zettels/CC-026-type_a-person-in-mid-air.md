TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

Darkness with a red-brown weight at its centre. Bodies in the air — falling, thrown, jumping, floating — arrive continuously from every direction and pass through one another without collision. There is no floor and no ceiling and no one lands. The same primitive force clearly governs all of it. Do not explain what the force is. Let a body occasionally slow almost to stillness before being taken again.

TENSION:
Exotic imagery can imitate an originary world without establishing a common force.
