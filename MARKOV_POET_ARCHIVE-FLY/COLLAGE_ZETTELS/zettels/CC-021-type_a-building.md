PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The milieu is a determined geo-historical situation whose forces are explicated through behaviour.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
One noun of the segmenter's vocabulary, as a picture.

LOCAL MOVE:
Grey-blue slabs and cornices pile into a single mass with a street-level implied at the bottom edge. It reads as a city because the cut pieces all came from the same height of camera.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The milieu survives the loss of every particular building. What persisted was the point of view of the street.

QUESTION:
How much historical determination must remain visible for a setting to be a milieu?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is a category an image, or only the shadow of the instrument that named it?

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A city that is only massing: grey-blue facades at every scale piled into one wall, with a strip of street-level activity along the bottom of frame. Follow one figure walking that strip. Every gesture — how far they keep from the wall, where they cross, what they look up at — should make the city's pressure more legible. Never show a whole building and never show the sky.

TENSION:
World-building describes a milieu; the action-image requires the situation to determine conduct.

MISSING:
