TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A road seen from a fixed height, running left to right across frame. Nothing travels the whole length. Instead, separate journeys occupy separate stretches of it — a cyclist here, a truck there, someone walking a dog further along — none of them ever meeting. Slowly it becomes clear they are all on the same road at the same time. One pale structure stands at the centre and every journey passes it.

TENSION:
A network of paths can juxtapose without generating a common world.

MISSING:

BOUNDARY:
