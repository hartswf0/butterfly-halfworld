ZETTEL

SOURCE:
David Deamer — Deleuze's Cinema Books — 2016 — Figure II.01, p. 78.
OUR IMAGE: cut/out/parts/P070.png — an uncut frame from shot P070. This is the state
every other image in this library is a departure from.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"central and privileged image"

[PARAPHRASE]
Solid perception is full composition: the world of the film is organised around
one privileged centre whose perception becomes dominant.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The plate before any cutting: a frame that still has a subject.

LOCAL MOVE:
The privileged image here is not a person. A light dominates and the figure is arranged around it.

SOURCE TERMS:
solid perception
central
privileged image
composition
centre

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
This is the only condition in the whole library that required no operation, and
it is the one that looks most like a decision.

QUESTION:
How much of a frame must become subordinate to one centre before perception is
solid?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is an uncut generated frame already centred, or does the centre arrive with the
prompt that asked for it?

MECHANISM:
world of images
→ generation from a prompt
→ a privileged body
→ the frame organised around it.

OUR FORMALIZATION:

TEXT-TO-CINEMA PROMPT:

A room in which a light — not a person — holds the centre. A figure moves through and is organised by it: shadowed, edge-lit, eclipsed. Never let the figure take the frame. The lamp is the perceiver.

TENSION:
A generated frame's centre was specified in language before it was seen, so its
solidity may be an artefact of the prompt rather than of the image.

MISSING:
A frame from this archive with no centre at all, for comparison.

BOUNDARY:
A well-composed shot ≠ solid perception.
