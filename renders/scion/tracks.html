FOOT TRACKS

Each foot's world position, plotted against time running down the page. The dashed line is the body. A planted foot stacks its samples into a vertical bar and then jumps a stride to the next one — a staircase beside a ramp. A skating foot never stacks: it draws a second ramp parallel to the body, which is what every walking shot in this suite was doing. Showing a window of the walk, not all of it: at full extent one stride is a few pixels.
