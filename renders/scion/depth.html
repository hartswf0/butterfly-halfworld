DEPTH, FROM THE CELS

The halfworld is one flat 192×144 field, so a camera move moves everything together — the one thing a camera move must not do. cineosis-lab already measured the missing structure: cels/build_scion.py carves the same suite into 166 cel shots and gives each a seven-plane stack with metric depth, a parallax coefficient and the contents of every plane, off the footage the poems were drawn from. Both sides are on the same 1440.07 s clock, so 91 of our 94 shots inherit a stack directly. The three that do not are the title cards, which the cel cut does not carry.

Two of these contradict the SCION prompt’s nominal ladder, and the measurement wins. Its P3 sits at Z 1.2, in front of a midground at 2.5 — measured, P3 is the second furthest plane, because what lands in it is street lamps and trees, which stand behind the middle of a room. And its stack runs to Z 6.0 at P5 where the footage says 2.66: these are interiors and streets, not landscapes. A parallax built on the nominal numbers would hold the background nearly still where the real one swings two thirds as far as the character.

The depth and parallax columns are separate medians and do not reconstruct each other. Within one cel shot parallax is exactly that shot’s character depth over the plane’s — 278 of 286 planes satisfy it to 0.02 — but every shot has a different character depth, so the median of the ratios is not the ratio of the medians. The parallax column is the authoritative one.

Drift in cells, against offset × parallax × amplitude × Δu. Worst error 0.46 of a cell on a one-cell lattice — the stack reproduces the cels’ parallax to within half a cell. Stated as a ratio instead it read as a 6% failure, which was the measure’s fault and not the stack’s.

A ROOM, IN DEPTH

The doorframe edge on P0 sweeps clean off frame at 1.643; the window on P4 opens outward; the body on P2 grows; the floor rake on P1 sweeps past; the far wall’s horizon barely moves. A plane here is not a second buffer — the world has no alpha and compositing would need one. It is a coordinate wrapper around the drawing kit, so the ink lands in the same single field and the halftone, the instance ids and the footage blend all keep working untouched.
