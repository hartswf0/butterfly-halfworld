VOICEBATH — one recording, one poem, several listeners, one field.

A·POEM — load the MP3, paste the poem with its line breaks. ALIGN BY TAP plays the recording; tap the pad (or space) on each word. WHISPER asks a model for its own boundaries. Both are kept.
B·LISTENERS — each listener writes claims into the ledger. ↻ re-runs one. BREATH vs LINE shows where the poet's pauses agree with the page, break mid-line, or run through a line end.
C·LEDGER — every claim: who heard it, what, when, how sure. Tap a word for its fingerprint. KIN finds words said with the same gesture, meaning ignored.
D·BATH — the listeners as rows on one clock. Drag to crop. Play the crop as the poet, or through the PUPPET, which re-utters it from the ledger alone. The red bar is wear: how far the puppet fell from the voice.
