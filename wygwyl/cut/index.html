A cut point you have not heard is a guess with a timecode. Clicking one auditions it: a second and a half either side, with the level dropped to nothing right where the cut would land. You are not listening to the music — you are listening for whether the hole shows. If it is invisible, the cut is free. If the music lurches, the sheet was right to score it.

The map is the song: spectrum on top, energy under it, then the rests as a band and the free cuts as ticks. White verticals are section boundaries — the places the song already decided something changed. Click anywhere on it to seek. Space plays, ← → step the cuts, ↑ ↓ change song.
