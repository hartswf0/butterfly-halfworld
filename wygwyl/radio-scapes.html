THE WATER, NOW

tide— flood— heart— mouth— out—

STATIONS

The waves are the instrument. Two or three wave systems of different lengths cross the frame; where they agree the crest darkens, and a darker mark is a louder note — construction and destruction are the score. The poem's own visualized sounds drift on the water as ridgelines; a vertical mark strikes once a beat — the IV drip, the heartbeat. Her reading plays whole and untouched: while she speaks the sea falls to a trickle and the waves flatten; in her silences the flood blooms far past politeness — louder and quieter at the same time, keyed to her breath. When she finishes, the other mouths sing her lines while the water disintegrates back to the tide.  ·  space plays, ← → change station.
