There is no audio file. The picture is rendered and the sound is synthesised from the same instant, thirty times a second, so they cannot drift — there is one clock and both of them are it. The bright column is the read head: it is the part of the frame you are hearing. A horizon sustains, a mast strikes, and a body walking left arrives earlier in each pass, which is a rhythm accelerating because someone is moving. Each film scans under its own prosodic engine, and the panel shows that law moving.  ·  space plays, ← → change station.
