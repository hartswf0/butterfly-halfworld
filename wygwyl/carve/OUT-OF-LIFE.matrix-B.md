# Diegesis

One body moves through six night locations and then off a ledge; the last third is uninterrupted descent.

**World rules**

- Every light source is practical and coloured: teal streetlamps, blue window glow, white steam, gold city windows.
- The body is backlit or hooded in every shot but the last; no face resolves until 01:01:22:00.
- Smoke or steam or fog is present in every location without exception.
- No object is ever transferred between entities. There is one entity.
- Camera holds behind or beside the body for 74 seconds, then goes below it and faces it.

**Entities** — MAN — heavyset, dark jacket, hood up from 01:00:34:12 onward

**Locations**

- WET ROAD — fog, teal lamps, magenta source right, standing water
- NEON PLAZA — wet ground, practical neon sign OUT OF LIFE
- BEDROOM — graffiti wall, bed, potted plant, green-teal window
- SMOKE ROOM — same window wall, blue, filled with vapour
- BRICK ALLEY — red brick, fire escape, white steam
- CRT ROOM — a monitor in the dark showing a burning street
- LEDGE — fire escape platform above a cloud
- FALL SPACE — radial gold streaks, no floor
