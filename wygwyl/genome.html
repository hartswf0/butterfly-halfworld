THE GENOME

due diligence on the sound experiments of the dig — what each one assumes, what each one demonstrably fails at, and which genes survive into whatever comes next. Written after four course corrections in one day, each of which was itself data. No salesmanship below: the failures are stated with the same confidence as the wins, and the builder's own wrong assumptions are the last section, because they caused more waste than any bug. · atlas

II · DUE DILIGENCE — experiment by experiment

1 · SONIC FIELD · sonicfield.html · terrain of 1,442 sounds, hovering is hearing

  • Assumes spatial neighborhood (UMAP) implies audible kinship — mostly true for CLAP, unverified for the DINO spectrogram layout.
  • Assumes a browser can hold 1,400 Audio elements gracefully — it can't; players leak, loops pile up under fast mouse travel.
  • Fails: exploration instrument only — it produces walks, not mixes; WALK AS SCORE exports collage, and collage was never once accepted as music here.
  • Fails: the ecology default clumps hard; the terrain reads as blobs at rest and only becomes legible in motion.

verdict: KEEP as a library door. Not a composition tool and stops claiming to be.

2 · POEM WORLDS + THE DESK · poemworlds.html · layers, orchestra, sidechain, mid-carve, depth

  • Assumes a world = simultaneous loops; richness = more layers. The correction "cluttered" was aimed exactly here.
  • Assumes ducking solves masking. It lowers collision; it does not create relation — nothing in a world listens to anything else.
  • Assumes strict CLAP referral defines belonging; when it starved, the orchestra was patched in by name/mood — two incompatible theories of kinship now cohabit one page.
  • Fails: static — every layer loops at constant identity; no arc, no memory, no decay. A world that never changes is a screensaver.
  • Fails: the ATL_LOOP samples can carry the poet's own buried voice — a second her under her, unlabeled.

verdict: MUTATE — the desk (sidechain, carve, depth, voxBus) is the keepable organ; the "world as parallel loops" body should be retired in favor of time-structured forms.

3 · RADIO ATLANTIS v1→v2 · score_engine.py · composed scores, measured loop, CLAP juror

  • Assumes SNR during words ≈ intelligibility. Disproven by the transmission: v2 passed 13 dB gates while the ear still lost words — the gate measured energy, not understanding.
  • Assumes the CLAP juror's two prompts span the quality axis; margin>0.02 is one binary opinion from a model that has never heard a good film mix.
  • Assumes acts-at-thirds is the poem's shape. It is a default, not a reading of any poem.
  • Fails: the revision vocabulary is only "quieter" — every failing station converged by turning things down, which is mastering, not composing.
  • Fails: ghosts/foley placement consumed silences the transmission later proved were the poem's most important material.

verdict: MUTATE — keep the loop skeleton and the honesty of the log; replace SNR with rewhisper as the primary gate; give REVISE verbs other than attenuation.

4 · VOICE CENSUS / HUNT / CODEX · voices.json, voicehunt.json, codex.json — the forensic line

  • Assumes MFCC+F0 separates speakers — false on mixes (census sil 0.24), serviceable on clean stems (hunt sil 0.29), never validated against ground truth by a human ear.
  • Assumes whisper's transcript of SUNG words is alignable text; codex coverage numbers (23–58% on some poems) are floor estimates distorted by ASR failure on melody, not by absent lines.
  • Fails: "100% of the words and the other voices" is not yet achieved anywhere: the codex holds 157 extracted lines of ~277 poem lines; New Day has zero carrier; the mortician and singers were found but never fully mapped line-for-line.
  • Fails: the poet/other-mouth voice-mark thresholds (poet<3.0) were set by eye on one distribution, not calibrated.

verdict: KEEP the data, distrust the coverage numbers downward-biased; the missing due diligence is a human pass over the 40 farthest strangers.

5 · DUETS / ONE CUT · the two voices woven, then spliced

  • Assumes whisper segment boundaries are cuttable edges. They are lexeme boundaries; the ear heard the difference immediately.
  • Assumes jaccard strength = dramatic strength when choosing "strong places" — a text-overlap number standing in for feeling.
  • Fails: the overlap elision ("echo") was praised in one context and ordered removed in another — the experiment had no way to know which context it was in.
  • Fails: extracted sung clips carry bleed from adjacent song material; a "line" is really a window.

verdict: MUTATE — the call-and-response idea survives; the implementation must inherit VOICE UNTOUCHED (whole takes, placement by playback, never by razor).

6 · THE PACING DESK · butterfly-desk.html — the acknowledged wrong turn

  • Assumed "the actual butterfly world" meant the feature film. It meant the WYGWYL worlds. The entire target was wrong.
  • Fails by scope, not mechanism — its genes (cut-list as cue sheet, snap to breath, one strip) transplanted cleanly into WORLD SCORE within the hour.

verdict: HOLD — a organ donor, kept in the atlas as labeled.

7 · WORLD SCORE · worldscore.html — lines into movements, hits on the worlds' cues

  • Assumes the reading can be cut per line and placed per movement — violates VOICE UNTOUCHED, re-imports the razor problem the transmission had just solved.
  • Assumes token overlap correctly pairs movement lines with timing segments; the observed match "welcome us. Poets who…" shows boundary smear.
  • Fails: her pacing is subordinated to the picture's — the exact inversion of "the audio must be first class," shipped hours before that correction arrived.

verdict: MUTATE — right film, wrong hierarchy. Invert it: her whole reading as the clock, the world's movements stretched to HER (the runtime already knows how to stretch to a record — the record should be the reading).

8 · THE TRANSMISSION · transmission.py — loops decaying, rewhisper gate

  • Assumes 85% word survival is enough — the brief says 100%; and whisper's own ceiling on the clean readings was never measured, so "92–99%" has no baseline to stand against.
  • Assumes her voice alone constitutes the words that matter; the other mouths are entirely absent from every station.
  • Assumes the mono, band-tilted broadcast voicing serves the material; nobody has confirmed the aesthetic against the actual references (Basinski's decays are stereo, enormous, slow).
  • Fails: the loop sources (drone + elements) are the same three chairs as everywhere else — the disintegration is real, but what disintegrates is thin.
  • Fails: first-pass 14/14 means the gate never bit — a gate that nothing fails is not yet known to be a gate.

verdict: KEEP + RAISE — measure the clean-reading baseline per poem, gate at ≥ baseline (that IS "100%": lose nothing whisper could ever hear), add the other mouths, and push one station until it fails to prove the gate has teeth.

9 · RADIO-SCAPES · the water on the bandbank

  • Assumes the flood envelope (0.10 under words) IS the mix — there is no rewhisper on a live page; her intelligibility over the bank is asserted by ducking depth, never measured.
  • Assumes the bandbank's mode-locked resynthesis reads as "water" — the crests are in tune by construction, which may be exactly the "beyond being in tune" the brief warned against.
  • Fails: the visualized-sound squiggles are decorative ink at ridgeline scale — 24-point contours carry almost no figure energy; the sounds' pictures are seen but barely heard.
  • Fails: the other mouths queue politely at the end; the brief's ecology wants them IN the water, and the current design has no way to voice a recording through the field.

verdict: KEEP AS TRUNK — it carries ONE CLOCK + VOICE UNTOUCHED + measurable dynamics on the only engine the ear has endorsed. Its named failures are the next work, not reasons to fork again.

10 · SUPPORTING ORGANS · clap-sketch, families, song observatory, joined field

  • Joined field assumes a shared 24-prompt vocabulary spans two models' semantics; the modalities islanded and the fix (re-homing sounds onto image centroids) makes the join partly cosmetic. Ruled "cluttered and hard to navigate" — correct.
  • CLAP-labeling assumes zero-shot labels are stable identities; they are one model's guess with a margin, and every downstream mood-match (orchestras, beds, scenes) inherits that softness silently.
  • Families fails for sampling (structure-grade, not sample-grade — already conceded) and nothing since has re-tested the segments.

verdict: HOLD — data infrastructure, not instruments; stop presenting them as destinations.

III · THE BUILDER'S OWN ASSUMPTIONS — the expensive ones

  • "More layers = richer." Cost: the cluttered field, the 19-layer worlds. The corpus's own films prove the opposite — eight ink levels, one accent, one thing at a time.
  • "A named referent is the referent." "Butterfly world" → wrong film. "Butterfly WYGWYL prompt" → wrong again until radio.html was pointed at directly. Cost: two full instruments. The fix is boring: confirm the target artifact by URL before building.
  • "Metrics I can compute are the qualities that matter." SNR stood in for hearing until rewhisper; jaccard stands in for strength; CLAP margin stands in for beauty. Each proxy was treated as the thing until the ear objected.
  • "Approval of a mechanism transfers to its next context." The doubled voice: good in radio, ordered removed in duets. Laws here are context-scoped and each new instrument must re-ask which dials it inherits.
  • "Shipping is understanding." Four corrections in one day says otherwise. This document exists because the genome was carried in commit messages and memory files instead of being a first-class, shared artifact.

IV · THE OPEN DEBTS — due diligence not yet done

  • No human listening ledger. Every gate is a machine proxy (whisper, CLAP, SNR). The single most valuable missing dataset is the poet's own per-station verdicts — the volholla_notes practice, applied to our renders. Until it exists, "passed" means "passed a proxy."
  • No clean-reading whisper baseline. "The radio kept 94%" is unanchored; whisper on the dry master might keep 95%. Measure the ceiling, gate against it.
  • No adversarial pass on any gate. Nothing has ever been pushed until it failed; gates that always pass are unproven instruments.
  • No line-level map of the other voices. 82 takes and 11 voices exist as clusters, not as a lyric-sheet of who says which line where — which is what "keep the other voices" actually requires.
  • The stems are still mostly idle. 34 songs separated; only bass/drums loops used. The isolated vocals — the cleanest other-mouth material we own — have never been placed in a single mix.

V · THE LATENT DESIGN — what everything points to and nothing built

Read the endorsements against the corrections and one organism appears behind the organs. It has five properties, each pointed at constantly, none ever built:

  • ONE SUBSTANCE. The census proved 83% of the speech is a few takes re-dressed; the codex proved the songs carry the whole poems; the drone is one continuous timeline. Everything is her voice at different states — and every mixer we built still says "voice + music" as if they were two things. The combining instruments failed because combining assumes two where there is one.
  • ONE CLOCK. The only gene the ear endorsed — and then every instrument invented a private timeline. Never built: the corpus-wide clock, where a moment addresses everything alive at that moment.
  • ALWAYS ON, NEVER RECORDED. "There is no audio file" is the law of the one page called charming. The transmission — named for it — is fourteen mp3s. Never built at corpus scale: the broadcast assembled live under laws, incapable of drift because it is never a performance of the idea.
  • AN ECOLOGY. "Hear what is being said by the other tracks." Nothing we built listens; ducking is avoidance, not hearing. Never built: recordings whose behavior is a function of each other's content — imitation, answer, retreat — construction and destruction from interaction, not placement.
  • IT COSTS. Basinski's loops matter because playback destroys them, and this corpus already IS disintegration loops — the same takes repeated 243 times in changing dress. Never built: persistence. No play has ever changed the archive. A broadcast with no fear, mercy or malice would erode what it repeats, so what survives is what was left alone.

And the interface we keep circling: tuning is navigation. Six dials exist; never built is the one dial across the whole corpus, where moving frequency moves through poems, mouths, songs, worlds and years — where excavation and listening are the same gesture.

In one sentence: a single, always-on, self-hearing, self-eroding broadcast of one voice in all her states, on one clock, navigated by tuning — Atlantis not resurrected once into files, but continuously, and spent by being heard. Its two prerequisites are exactly the two debts of section IV: the shared clock, and persistence.

The genome's summary, honestly: the proven spine is ONE CLOCK + VOICE UNTOUCHED + MEASURED GATE (rewhisper) + SNAP TO BREATH. Everything that repeatedly failed shares one gene: the razor. Everything that repeatedly disappointed shares one assumption: that a proxy could stand where an ear should be.
