SOURCE

The two places a model would attach — HEAR (read the sound-map back to you) and VOICE→SKETCH (draw what it heard) — are offline here. VOICE→FIELD does the second without one: the envelope is already a curve, so it is written straight into the field as ground.
The clock is the recording. Not a metronome and not a master track — the poet's own currentTime, and everything reads it. The drawing is timed: every point carries the moment you made it, so a stroke made at 0:42 is not on the field at 0:41. You do not draw a picture of the voice, you draw along with it, and the mark performs again when the record does.

And the field is the spectrum — rows are frequency, columns are time, ink is amplitude — so the stroke sounds as you make it, with no key and no round trip. The six relations are rules rather than instructions: HARMONIZE thins while the voice speaks and fills when it rests; ANSWER enters only in what the voice left, and the amber bands on the world panel are exactly that material; SHADOW doubles quietly and late; COUNTERPOINT runs its own line and is rewarded when it happens to meet; RESIST holds a pulse against free speech and never quantises the voice; EMBODY changes the picture instead of the score, and the picture is what makes the sound.  ·  space plays · 0–7 pick a level · click the world panel to seek.
