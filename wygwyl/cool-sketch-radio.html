LAYERS — TOP OF THE LIST IS ON TOP

Analysis and playback share one law. row r ↔ LO·(HI/LO)^((143−r)/143), 140 Hz at the bottom to 7168 at the top. A sound dropped in is transformed through it and quantised to eight levels; the read head crosses the field and resynthesises through it. So a patch laid where it was analysed plays back as itself, and every collage move is a signal operation you can see: up is higher, right is later, wide is slower, tall opens the intervals out, level is quieter, and a hole cut in it is a band gone silent. Those are not metaphors for each other — they are the same thing said twice.

A patch stops remembering where it came from. A voice, a song, six seconds of the suite's own record, a frame of any of the fourteen films, or a rectangle you paint by hand — once it is in the field it is eight-level ink, and it will be sounded whether it began as a picture or as a sound. Drop a shed roof into the top of the frame and it is a chord.
