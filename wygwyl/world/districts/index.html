Colour onto time: the album as a colour script

Each passage of the album (24 minutes, the drone record's clock) split into its set pieces, each coloured by the district it happens in; under it, the hour the treatment gives it. Night runs from 01 to 10, the ride crosses to dawn, the shore holds the day, and the haze takes it back to black.

Transitions: the cuts between poems are places

Every departure in the treatments is a mode (fall, fly, cut, walk, lift, ride, dissolve, door). Where the mode is physical it needs a place in the city between two districts; where it is not, the black holds it. The views that sit between two districts in CLIP are the bridges, and three of them are exactly these cuts.
