WHERE YOU GO WHEN YOU LEAVE

The Island

Fourteen poems, each a set: the room at the top of the tower and its fire escape, the childhood house, the chapel on the shore, the night beach, the meadow of the ancestors, the Light Gates, the party, the rooftop, the phone booth, the motorcycle, the white temple, the hall, the café, the old door in the black. Drive between them, get out, walk in. Each keeps its poem’s hour — the light walks with you. Frame the shots, play the scene against the reading, cut it from the staged set and the generated stills.
