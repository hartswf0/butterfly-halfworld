A landscape for every set of shots

Every set of images in the archive — each poem against each source: the MARKOV_POET clips, the generated stills, the archive footage, the Halfworld renders, the film, everything else — grows a world of its own. What the set shows, taken together, becomes the genome: water decides the coast (a harbour where water meets city, an ocean shore where it meets sand); city and street decide how many more cities, how dense, how tall; rock above the horizon raises the mountains; forest and meadow set the nature. Three worlds are grown and the set chooses the one that best gives its own shots back and that a builder would still build; then that world is sculpted toward each shot and re-shot.
