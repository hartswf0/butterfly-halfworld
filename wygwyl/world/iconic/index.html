The imageable city

Kevin Lynch asked people to draw their city from memory and found five things in every drawing: the paths they move along, the edges that bound them, the districts they feel inside of, the nodes where they gather, and the landmarks they steer by. We have thousands of shots; each one is a cognitive map. Here the canon — the most iconic shots in the archive, chosen the same way every time — is read for those five things, and the city is drawn back out of them. Every mark on this map is held by the shots that see it: tap it.

Triangles: landmarks, sized by how many shots hold them (gold: real, named ones). Rings: nodes at the poems’ places. Heavy lines: the journey’s paths. Hatched bands: edges. Dotted rings: districts.
