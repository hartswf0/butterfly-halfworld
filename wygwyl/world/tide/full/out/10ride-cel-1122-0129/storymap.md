# Diegesis
A cel-shaded animated world where a Black man wearing a black turtleneck, black pants, and black boots rides a black motorcycle across abruptly shifting urban landscapes—from a dark street under a single streetlamp, to a wet neon-lit commercial road, through a monochromatic grey concrete tunnel and foggy cityscape, and down a dusk street hung with yellow lanterns and lined with East/Southeast Asian onlookers in teal, green, and orange garments. The only audio is a male voiceover reciting a poem ('world sound not observed').
