How to read it

NOUNS

What the segmenter could name is written in its name: SKY, WATER, CITY, PERSON. What it could not name is written in the line he is saying. The machine supplies the nouns; he supplies the rest.

HIS LINE

The whole picture is written in the line that produced it, over and over, like a typewriter filling a page.

The waterline

Rises through each picture while its passage is read. Above it: dry noise from the picture before. Below it: the new picture, legible.

Colour

Every wet letter takes the colour of the pixel under it. Brighter pixels are set bolder. In PAPER the same data is typed in ink: dark areas heavy, light areas blank.
