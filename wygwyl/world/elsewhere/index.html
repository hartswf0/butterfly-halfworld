No city was built first. Each shot is folded from its own segmentation into floor, walls and sky, and you can walk a few steps into it. Its exits are other shots: forward is what the middle of the frame becomes, left/right what continues past its edge, rhyme the same spatial event in an unlike subject. The map above is drawn from walking those links; where two walks disagree the place is kept twice (dashed). the built city · atlas
