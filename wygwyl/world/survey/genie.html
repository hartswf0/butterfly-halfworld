Through the Door

Genie 4 turns one picture and one sentence into a place you can walk through for two minutes, with sound. Here is how I think that fits Atlantis, in nine panels.

What I would build first

Three small experiments

  • Door test. One hero building, one seed frame, twenty 60-second walks through the door. Sort the interiors that come back; see what recurs.
  • District beds. One walk per district with the audio saved. Compare against the duets for tone.
  • Clock test. Same seed, same route, four text events: dawn, noon, dusk, flood. That is the day cycle the 3D maps lack.

Where it does not fit

  • It cannot hold the city. Two minutes, 720p, no memory between sessions — the Island or its successor still has to be the persistent map.
  • Finished shots still go through an offline video model; the model page points to Gemini Omni for editing and upscaling.
  • It streams about 5,760 output tokens a second, so cost per walk needs checking against the pricing page before running hundreds.
  • I have read only the model page, not the two guides it links, and have not run a session.
